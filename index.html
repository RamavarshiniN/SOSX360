What happens after you press submit
  1. 01You report

    Voice or text, in your language. Location is pinned on the map.

    ~30 s
  2. 02Severity is rated

    Category, people affected and words like "trapped" set Low to Critical.

    instant
  3. 03Ranked

    A priority score that also grows with waiting time, so nothing is forgotten.

    live
  4. 04Nearest team

    Closest available team of the right type, with road distance and ETA.

    1 click or auto
  5. 05Tracked

    Assigned, In Progress, Resolved. You follow it with your INC number.

    until closed
